title: "Full screen and slideshow"
group: "Looking"
order: 4
summary: "The image on black, the pointer and the controls out of the way; a slideshow at the speed you set; and an Escape that knows which platform it is on."
---

- **Full screen** with <kbd>F</kbd> or <kbd>F11</kbd> (and the green button or <kbd>⌃⌘F</kbd> on a Mac): the image on black, the pointer hidden once it rests.
- **Slideshow** with <kbd>F5</kbd>: the folder in its order, full screen, every 1 to 60 seconds as set in Settings.
- **Escape leaves**, and on Linux closes the window when there is nothing to leave, as GNOME's viewer does. On macOS and Windows it never closes the window.
- **Floating controls** for previous, next and zoom appear over the image when the pointer moves, and fade when it rests, after a delay you choose.

See [Viewing](/docs/viewing).
