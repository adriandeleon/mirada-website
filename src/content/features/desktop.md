- **A header bar** laid out as GNOME Image Viewer's, drawn in the title bar; the title on the left on Windows, as its own title bars have it.
- **Windows**: the system accent colour, "Move to Recycle Bin", an MSI that carries its own Visual C++ runtime.
- **Linux**: the freedesktop.org Trash, written by Mirada itself so it can put files back; deb, rpm and tar.gz for x64 and ARM64, from Debian 12 and Ubuntu 22.04 up; `mirada` on your path from the deb.
- **Opens what you double-click**, on both: the installers register Mirada for every format it reads.
