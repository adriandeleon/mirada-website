summary: "A real menu bar with Preview's keys, Finder's double-click and Open With, the system accent colour, and a trackpad that pans and swipes."
---

- **The menu bar**: File, Image, View, Go and Help hold every command with its ⌘ key, ticks on what is on, and Help's search finds any command by name. About and Settings are in the Mirada menu.
- **Finder opens files in it**: a double-click, Open With, or a file dropped on the Dock icon, at launch and while it runs. (On an Intel Mac, only while it runs.)
- **A trackpad pans and swipes**: pinch to zoom, two fingers to move about, a sideways swipe to step.
- **The system's accent colour** for selections, followed as it changes.
- **Apple silicon gets the native build**; Intel Macs a trained Java runtime.
