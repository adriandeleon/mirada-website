title: "Light, dark, or the system's"
group: "Your desktop"

summary: "Follow the system's light or dark setting as it changes, or choose one. The floating controls stay dark pills over the photo either way."
---

- **Three choices** in Settings: follow the system, light or dark, applied at once.
- **Follows as it changes**, when it follows the system.
- **Controls on the photo** are the same translucent dark pills on either theme, because they sit on the picture, not the window.
