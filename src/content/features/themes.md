---

- **Six choices** in Settings: follow the system, light, dark, high contrast, Editora Light or Editora Dark, applied at once.
- **Editora's pair**, the "Caret & Ink" themes of Editora, with their own teal accent.
- **Follows as it changes**, when it follows the system.
- **Controls on the photo** are the same translucent dark pills on either theme, because they sit on the picture, not the window.
