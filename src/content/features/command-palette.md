summary: "Every command by name, with its key and a line on what it does, matched as you type. Greyed, with the reason, when it cannot run. Opened by keys you choose."
---

- **<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+P</kbd>, <kbd>Alt+X</kbd> or <kbd>F1</kbd>** (<kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>, <kbd>⌥X</kbd> or <kbd>F1</kbd> on a Mac).
- **Fuzzy matching**: letters in order, not necessarily together, ranked by how well they fit.
- **Says why not.** Next Image on the last image is greyed, with "This is the last image."
- **Emacs keys inside**: <kbd>Ctrl+N</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd> to move, <kbd>Ctrl+G</kbd> to cancel, and the editing keys in the field.
- **Also over images and folders**: Go to Image (<kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd>) and Open Recent (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+O</kbd>).
- **Your keys.** Settings → Keys → Command palette, or `palette.keys` in the settings file.
- **The same palette** as Editora and Termina.

See [Keyboard and the palette](/docs/keyboard).
