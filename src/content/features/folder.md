title: "The folder is the document"
group: "The folder"

summary: "Open one photo and the rest of the folder is there, in the order you'd number it: IMG_9 before IMG_10. Or by date or size, either way round."
---

- **Opening a file opens its folder**, starting at that file; opening a folder starts at its first image. Hidden files are left out.
- **Natural name order**: numbers inside names are counted, so `IMG_9` comes before `IMG_10`.
- **By date or size**, and **reversed**, from the palette, the right-click menu or Settings. The choice is remembered.
- **Where you are**: the header names the folder, and the sidebar says "4 of 8".
- **Open Recent** (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+O</kbd>): the last ten folders, found by name in the palette.

See [The folder](/docs/the-folder).
