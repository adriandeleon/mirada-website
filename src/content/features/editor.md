---
title: "Crop, turn and flip"
group: "Changing a photo"
order: 1
summary: "A small editor for the quick fix: crop freely or to a shape, turn and flip, undo any step, and save a new JPEG, PNG, TIFF or BMP. The original is left alone."
---

- **<kbd>E</kbd> opens it** over the image, already upright.
- **Crop** freely or to Original, Square, 5:4, 4:3, 3:2 or 16:9 (<kbd>A</kbd> cycles); drag the box or move it with the arrows.
- **Turn and flip**: <kbd>R</kbd>, <kbd>Shift+R</kbd>, <kbd>H</kbd>, <kbd>V</kbd>.
- **Undo everything**: <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Z</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl+Y</kbd>, Reset (<kbd>Backspace</kbd>) included. A drag or a run of arrow presses is one step.
- **Save a copy** (<kbd>Enter</kbd>): "name (edited)" beside the original, written to the side and moved into place. HEIC, AVIF, JPEG XL and WebP save as JPEG or PNG.
- **Instant on a preview**, saved from the full image.

See [Editing](/docs/editing).
