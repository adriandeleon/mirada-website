---
title: "The thumbnail strip"
group: "The folder"
order: 3
summary: "The folder along the bottom or down the side, as pictures, pictures with names, or names alone. Filled from the camera's own small copies, four to fifty times faster."
---

- **<kbd>T</kbd> opens it**, closed by default; click a thumbnail to show it, or drag it out to drop its file elsewhere.
- **Fast.** A camera's or phone's JPEG carries a small copy of itself; the strip shows that when it is large enough and the photo's shape, instead of decoding the photo.
- **What you see first.** Up to four are made at once, the ones on screen first, and one scrolled past before it was made is never made.
- **Three layouts**: at the side instead of along the bottom, names under the pictures, or names instead of pictures for a folder of thousands.

See [The folder](/docs/the-folder#the-thumbnail-strip).
