title: "Zoom, pan and turn"
group: "Looking"
order: 3
summary: "Fit, actual size and the steps between, a wheel that zooms about the pointer, crisp pixels above 200 %, and a zoom that can stay put while you step through a burst."
---

- **Fit or actual size.** <kbd>0</kbd> fits the window, <kbd>1</kbd> shows one image pixel per screen pixel, and a double-click toggles between them. A small image is never blown up to fill the window.
- **Zoom about the pointer.** The wheel keeps the spot under it in place; <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> step about the centre.
- **Crisp above 200 %.** Pixels are drawn as squares rather than smoothed, so you see what the file holds.
- **Pan** by dragging, with <kbd>Shift+←</kbd> <kbd>Shift+→</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>, or with two fingers on a Mac trackpad, where a pinch zooms and a swipe steps.
- **Keep Zoom When Stepping** (<kbd>Z</kbd>): compare the same detail across a burst of shots.
- **Rotate the view** with <kbd>R</kbd> and <kbd>Shift+R</kbd>, without touching the file.
